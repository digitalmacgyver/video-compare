# Snell & Wilcox Test Chart #2 (NTSC) — Cell-Level Layout

This document records the chart's per-cell content as observed in real captures
and cross-referenced against the upstream chart spec. Used by `tp_chart.py` and
related modules to decide where it is safe to place affine-fit landmarks and
where measurement regions should be sampled.

**Companion machine-readable file:** `docs/sw2_chart_layout.json`.

## Coordinate convention

- The chart picture is **720 × 486** pixels (NTSC SDI active raster).
- It is overlaid with a **12 × 9 grid** of cells, each **60 × 54 px**.
- Cells are named **`(row, col)`** with row 1 at the top, col 1 at the left.
- Grid-line intersections are at `((col-1)*60, (row-1)*54)` so the intersection
  at the bottom-right of cell `(r, c)` is at `(c*60, r*54)`.
- The chart's content (grid lines, features, labels) is overlaid on a grey
  background at 50% IRE (Y10 ≈ 502).

## Chart variants

We have seen two distinct visual variants of the chart in real captures.
Detection code that locks onto the chart's features must know which variant
it is processing.

### `standard` — native TPG output (SDI captures)

Generated by a Snell & Wilcox TPG20/TPG21 (or equivalent) and captured
directly off SDI. The chart we describe in the rest of this document is the
standard variant. Notably:

- Boundary arrows are **black arrowheads on the grey background**.
- The big circle is a thin **black ring** (168 ns ≈ 3 px).
- All measurement features (tartan, gray, bursts) are rendered at their
  native composite-quality levels.

Captures observed: `tpgSw2_composite_snellhdSnellGroup_pedIn_sdi.avi`,
`tpgSw2_composite_snellldRack_pedIn_sdi.avi`.

### `dvd` — DVD-distributed re-rendering

Distributed on DVD media. Visually similar to the standard chart but with
some inverted-polarity rendering choices, likely a result of being re-
authored as a video asset rather than passed straight from TPG. The
critical difference for our detectors:

- Boundary arrows are **WHITE arrowheads on BLACK regions** (contrast
  inverted vs the standard chart).
- The chart-identification text in cells (2,6) / (2,7) reads `525 D1`
  (the DVD's encoding identification) instead of `SW2 NTSC`.
- Several other labels in the upper region appear to be rendered on
  small black backplates with white type rather than the standard's
  white type directly on grey.
- The interior pattern of the chart (tartan, grays, bursts, zone plate,
  S&W banner, big circle) still follows the standard layout.

**Detection implication:** `tp_register._detect_boundary_triangle` uses a
"`Y < threshold`" dark-pixel search. **It will not find the DVD chart's
arrows.** Until the detector is taught to handle both polarities (or a
chart-variant flag is added), Stage 2 boundary-triangle detection should
be **skipped entirely on DVD sources** — fall back to grid landmarks +
circle + cross for registration and geometry derivation.

Captures observed: `sw2_dvdrip_sample.mov`.

### Detecting the variant automatically

The cleanest heuristic is to probe the rectangle just outside one of the
boundary-arrow positions:
- Sample the small region around cell-border location (180, 27) in cell
  (1, 3-4 border) — i.e., where the top-left arrow should be.
- If the dominant pixel value is dark (Y10 < 200), the variant is
  `standard`.
- If the dominant pixel value is near-zero (Y10 ≈ 0..50, a black region
  surrounding white arrows), the variant is `dvd`.

This auto-detection has not yet been implemented; for now operators can
pass a `--variant dvd` flag (to be added).

## Moving zone plate

- **Reserved container:** cells **(3,4) – (6,9)** — a 360 × 216 px rectangle
  centered approximately at chart picture-center.
- **Pattern radius (per chart spec):** 0.15 × picture_height = 0.15 × 486 ≈
  **73 px**.
- The pattern is a Fresnel zone plate — concentric circular zebra/moiré
  arcs — used to test motion-handling artifacts in comb decoders / scan
  converters.
- **The pattern moves frame-to-frame**; its exact instantaneous position is
  non-deterministic. For safety, treat the entire reserved container as a
  "do-not-sample" zone for any per-frame measurement.

## Cells safe for affine-fit landmarks

A grid intersection at `(c*60, r*54)` is "clean" if all 4 cells adjacent to it
contain only the grey background + grid lines (and possibly the thin circle
outline, provided the intersection is ≥ 30 px from the circle ring at radius
243 from picture center).

By the row-by-row table above, the cells that contain only grey + grid (no
text, burst, or other feature) are:

**Pure-grey cells:** (2,1), (2,4), (2,9), (2,12), (3,12), (7,1) (presumed),
(7,4) – (7,9) (presumed), (7,12) (presumed), (8,1) (presumed), (8,9), (8,12) (presumed), (9,4), (9,9).

**Cells with only the circle outline (otherwise pure grey):** (2,3), (2,10),
(3,3), (3,11), (4,2), (4,11), (5,2), (5,11), (6,2), (6,11), (7,2 — but also
has 4.28 label), (7,3), (7,10), (7,11), (8,3), (8,10).

A grid intersection is *fully* clean only when **all 4 adjacent cells are
either pure-grey or circle-only AND the intersection is > 30 px from the
circle ring**. Candidate intersections that meet this need verification
against the actual capture using `tp_sample_overlay.py`.
